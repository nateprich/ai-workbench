# Sources, licenses, and modifications

This is Nate Rich's integration, dated 2026-11-05. It contains deliberate adaptations of the original-source skills below. The shared conversation contract in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) controls routing, scope, interaction, persistence, and completion. No claim is made that the adaptations behave identically to their upstreams or are endorsed by their authors.

Nate's original integration is covered by [LICENSE](LICENSE), copied from the repository's MIT license. Retained upstream notices apply to the adapted material. In particular, `references/argument-audit.md` contains Apache-2.0-derived material under the [Apache license](licenses/sammcj-Apache-2.0.txt); the MIT integration license does not replace those terms.

## Method notices

`references/premortem.md` is newly written integration guidance with a short original description of Gary Klein's method and a citation to [Performing a Project Premortem](https://hbr.org/2007/09/performing-a-project-premortem), Harvard Business Review, September 2007. Article text, tables, and larger third-party premortem packages are not reproduced.

For the comparison adaptation, the relevant [radarist upstream notice](https://github.com/radarist/structured-analytic-skills/blob/d503ff5b164a6bed567a9c48214fd0517cad64dd/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md#5-methods-described-not-reproduced) distinguishes its own descriptions from protected expressions of published methods and credits Thomas L. Saaty for AHP. This pack retains its MIT notice but imports no AHP tables, matrices, scripts, or book passages. The upstream skill also credits Keeney/Raiffa's additive value model, Heuer/Pherson's decision matrix, and Belton/Stewart's sensitivity analysis. No unrelated third-party material from that repository is included.

At the pinned sammcj revision, inspection of the complete recursive tree found no root or skill-specific NOTICE applicable to the argument-audit source. The complete root Apache-2.0 license is included. No author attribution was invented where the source did not supply one.

## Maintaining this pack

Updates are deliberate, not live upstream dependencies. For an upstream update, inspect the exact changed source and applicable license/notices; update the revision and adaptation notes here; review how changes interact with the shared contract; rebuild the archive and rerun affected cold-start cases. Keep all license files with redistributed copies. Full upstream tools or companion files would require a separate scope/dependency review rather than silently expanding these adaptations.
